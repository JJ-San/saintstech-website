Before you ask

The questions owners actually ask.

What happens to our data?

Before anything runs, we set safety rules with you: what can go into AI, and what never does. Those rules are written down, and your team is trained on them.

What if it breaks after you leave?

Every build ships with a defect warranty and an ongoing support plan: monitoring, fixes, and updates when your business changes.

Will we be locked into you?

No. Every build is documented, with user guides and an operations manual, and your team is trained to run it without us.

Do you work outside Auckland?

STAS is Auckland-based and works with businesses across New Zealand. The first talk is free either way.

Is AI actually ready for this?

For some jobs, yes; for others, no. Telling you which is which is the entire point of the assessment. If the honest answer is "you don't need us", that's what it will say.
