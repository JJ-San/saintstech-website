About

The person who does the work.

STAS is deliberately a one-person firm. The engineer who scopes your job is the one who builds it, tests it, trains your team, and answers the phone afterwards.

I'm Josiah Santos, STAS's founder — an engineer who got tired of watching good businesses lose hours to jobs a machine should be doing.

I run STAS from Auckland, and the work is hands-on: I come to you, learn how your business actually runs, and put AI to work on the parts that don't need a human — properly, with testing, documentation, and a warranty behind it.

No sales team, no juniors, no handoffs. If we work together, you deal with me.
